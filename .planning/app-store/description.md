DinnerTime answers "what's for dinner?" in one photo.

Open your fridge. Snap a picture. DinnerTime tells you what to cook with
what you already have — no more staring at ingredients trying to connect
the dots.

WHAT IT DOES

- SNAP + SUGGEST. Take one photo of your fridge or pantry. DinnerTime
  recognizes ingredients and offers three dinner ideas that fit your
  kitchen, your cooking skill, and your preferences.
- PLAN THE WEEK. Tap a suggestion to pin it to a day. See the whole week
  at a glance — protein rotation, cuisine variety, repeat meals, all
  automatic.
- HANDS-FREE COOKING. When you're actually at the stove, just talk to
  DinnerTime. "How do I know the chicken is done?" "Start a 10-minute
  timer." Real conversation, no screen taps.
- GROCERY IN ONE TAP. Missing ingredients? Tap once, open Instacart with
  a pre-populated cart. No list-copying, no guesswork.

BUILT FOR REAL KITCHENS

- Works with what's actually in your fridge — not a curated demo pantry.
- Respects dietary restrictions, allergies, and household size from day
  one.
- Skill-aware — beginners get 30-minute dinners, pros get weekend
  projects.
- Grows with you — DinnerTime learns your cooking patterns and
  progresses.

Powered by Anthropic Claude for vision and reasoning. Your photos are
processed and discarded, never stored. See our privacy policy for
details.

Get dinner decided. Tonight.
