The 5-Day-a-Week Tax

Every household hits the same wall at 6pm: "What do we have? What do we make? Where's the list?" It's not a recipe problem — it's a decision problem, repeated 260 times a year. Meal-kit services charge $120/week to remove it. Recipe apps don't solve it. DinnerTime does.

The Magic Loop

1 Snap — one photo of the fridge or pantry shelf.
2 Claude Vision identifies every item, auto-routes to fridge/freezer/pantry, dedupes against what's already there.
3 Suggest — 3 dinners you can actually make, ranked by what uses up what's expiring.
4 Plan — tap once, 7-day meal plan populated.
5 Order — missing items auto-forwarded to Instacart. Cart is pre-built.
6 Cook — hands-free voice guidance walks you through it, no screen taps with greasy hands.

Zero mental effort from "what do we have?" to "what should we cook?"

Why Now

  • Vision-class AI got cheap. Claude Sonnet 4 identifies ingredients from a blurry fridge shot at ~$0.003/scan. Two years ago this was a research paper.
  • Instacart opened its API. Link-based cart injection means zero fulfillment risk — we're a demand-gen layer, they close the loop.
  • On-device speech finally works well enough for hands-free cooking without a cloud round-trip.
  • Meal-kit fatigue. HelloFresh / Blue Apron churn is at all-time highs. Consumers want the cognitive relief without the $12/meal premium.

Technical Moat

AI VISION
Single-shot pantry capture — Claude vision model parses every item in one photo. No bounding-box UX, no "scan each item" friction.
SEMANTIC DB
Canonical ingredient graph on pgvector — "organic chicken breast" and "chicken breast" merge into one pantry row. 366 canonical ingredients, 1,587 aliases shipped. Admin-promoted from aggregate scan data.
PERSONALIZATION
Learned placement rules — app observes user overrides ("milk goes in fridge") and suggests rules after 3 repetitions. Aggressive auto-accept for user-flagged "staples" (milk, eggs) even on low-confidence scans.
VOICE
On-device STT + Claude + TTS pipeline for cooking mode. No wake-word latency, works without signal, privacy-friendly.
ARCHITECTURE
Zero-key mobile — all AI/Instacart calls go through our Hono backend proxy. No API keys exposed, full spend control, eval harness for prompt regressions. Supabase handles auth/storage/realtime in one dependency.
